## Written by Florin

GPL-2.0-or-later (`LICENSE-GPL-V2`, `LICENSE-GPL-V3`):

- the firmware additions: `firmware/handheld/src/core/on_demand.rs` (on-demand core files: save states for the cores
  on the SD card), `core/cheats.rs` (cheats from `.cht` files), `util/atomic_file.rs` (safe saves, `.bak`),
  `util/localtime.rs` (time zone), `res/ui/theme.slint` (themes), `res/ui/components/scrolling_text.slint`. They are
  part of a GPL-3.0-only program, so the firmware as a whole is GPL-3.0-only;
- the Game Boy save-state walker `fpga/src/main/scala/net/gamebub/core/gameboy/GbSaveStateWalker.scala`, the Game Boy
  test simulator `fpga/sim/test_main.cpp` and `scripts/rev2/gb_test_sim.sh`;
- the LCD aspect scaler (used by cores on the SD card): `fpga/src/main/scala/platform/handheld/LcdAspectScaler.scala`,
  `lib/video/PolyphaseScaler.scala`, `net/gamebub/framework/interface/VideoScalerV0.scala` and its wiring in
  `HandheldTop.scala`, `fpga/src/test/scala/platform/handheld/LcdAspectScalerSpec.scala`;
- `fpga/src/test/scala/lib/mem/sdram/BurstSdramControllerSpec.scala`;
- the build scripts `scripts/rev2/make_vivado_bundle.py`, `pack_vivado_bundles.py`, `finish_bitstream.py`,
  `make_system_data.sh`;
- the "Retro Bub" logo (pixel art; the letters "e" and "Bub" keep the pixels of the original Game Bub logo's font):
  `firmware/handheld/res/logo.png` and the two theme masks `logo-face.png`, `logo-shadow.png`, the boot design's
  `fpga/src/main/resources/logo.png`, all generated by `scripts/rev2/make_logo.py`.

Florin's changes to Eli Lipsitz's files (the Game Boy core fixes, the GBA Classic NES Series support, the firmware's
`core/`, `ui/`, `bitstream/` and `device/` modules, `HandheldTop.scala`, `XpmFifoAsync.scala`, `build_core.py`, ...)
are under those files' licenses.
